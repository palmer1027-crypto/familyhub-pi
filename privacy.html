Family Hub Pi Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

What we access

When a family member connects their Google account, Family Hub Pi requests access to Google Calendar so it can read calendar events and display them on the household's kiosk screen.

How data is used and stored

Calendar event data is stored only on the family's own Raspberry Pi inside the home. It is used solely to display the family schedule. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Family Hub Pi's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect Google Calendar from the kiosk's Manage page, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting removes the stored tokens from the device.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on this site's repository.
